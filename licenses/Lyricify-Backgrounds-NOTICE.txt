Changes: translated the three artwork layer rotation periods and transforms,
classic saturation coefficients, and dark lyrics material to MyGo's Kage
shader language. Samples MySpotify's existing preblurred artwork, uses a
soft layer overlap, and draws to a reduced-size cached canvas at 10 FPS.
The original WPF/WinUI host, Gaussian blur and mesh implementation are not
embedded in this adaptation. Copyright notices and the Apache-2.0 license
are retained in Lyricify-Backgrounds-LICENSE.txt.
